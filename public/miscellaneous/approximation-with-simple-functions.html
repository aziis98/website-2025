Simple Function Approximation

Visualizing the theorem: Any bounded measurable function can be uniformly approximated by a monotone increasing sequence of simple functions.

How to use:
  • Draw a curve in the top plot area on the right.
  • Adjust n below to change the step precision.
  • Observe how the level sets $E_k$ decompose the domain below.
n = 1
Coarse Fine
Step size: $1/2^1 = 0.5$
Approximation formula:
$$ \phi_n(x) = \dfrac{\lfloor 2^n f(x) \rfloor}{2^n} = \sum_{k=0}^{2^n - 1} \dfrac{k}{2^n} \chi_{E_k}(x) $$
Original $f(x)$
Simple $\phi_n(x)$
Level Sets Decomposition Sets $E_k = \{x \mid \frac{k}{2^n} \leq f(x) < \frac{k+1}{2^n}\}$
