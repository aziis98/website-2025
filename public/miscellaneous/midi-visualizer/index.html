MIDI Visualizer

Play MIDI files in the browser with a nice real-time visualization inspired by samizdat's fuguestate. Drag and drop a MIDI file or click to select one from your computer. For now this works best with simple MIDI files of at most at most a few thousand notes (less than 10kB files).

or try a sample like super mario theme or pirate of the caribbean (loaded directly from BitMidi).

Find free MIDI files

BitMidi (~113k general/retro), Mutopia Project (public-domain classical), MidiWorld (classic archive), VGMusic (video game by console), Cymatics free / Unison free (royalty-free packs), BitMidi source (raw archive links).

Inspiration

This project was inspired by samizdat drafting co. / fuguestate, a visual exploration of Bach's musical compositions. His original midi file can be downloaded from his website at this link: bwv-578.mid (even if for the current architecture of the visualizer this may be a bit laggy).
