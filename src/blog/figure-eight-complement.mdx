The [figure eight knot](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure-eight_knot_(mathematics)>) is the simplest [hyperbolic knot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_link) (i.e. one that has a complement that admits a complete metric of constant negative curvature). One way to understand this structure is through an ideal triangulation of the knot complement.
